# Seasonal Builder — where SUI had no part

The `seasonal-builder` workshop bench (`dev/showcases/workshop/seasonal-builder.tsx`,
its pure model `seasonal-builder-model.ts`, its kit `seasonal-builder-kit/`) was
built under the push-back gate: nothing under `src/` was added or changed. Where
no SUI component fit, the bench wrote a small local piece (all zero-CSS
compositions of SUI parts; the two raw-SVG spots are named below) and recorded
the gap here. **Nothing below is approved.** Each row is a proposal for Peter to
accept, reshape or reject; a bench is not a consumer, so none of these should be
built until a shipping screen needs it.

| # | Gap | What the bench did instead | Proposed SUI component or prop |
|---|-----|----------------------------|--------------------------------|
| 1 | **A stacked band that can be hovered, selected, painted or hatched.** `StackedAreaSeries` paints only from the series palette and takes no hover, selection, click or hatch; the sketch dims the others on hover, outlines the selected season, hatches a projected stop's last week and prints the name on the band's longest run. | `StackedAreaSeries` for the bands (`curve="linear"`, so a season starts and stops square on its day), then two `AreaSeries` (`lower`) overlays: a translucent accent tint over the selected season and a `HatchPattern` fill over each projected tail. The strip's click is `Chart.onPick` (an x only), so it picks the topmost season on that day (`topSeasonOn`). No hover dimming and no names on the bands (each band has a `<title>`). Kit: `year-strip.tsx`. | `StackedAreaSeries` props: `selectedId`, `hoveredId`, `onBandClick` / `onBandHover`, and a per-band `emphasis` (`{ from, to }` day range drawn hatched). Names on a band would be a display component like `SpanLanes`' adornments. |
| 2 | **A labelled pin.** `PinMarkers` draws a glyph; a holiday on the calendar needs a stem down to the axis and its name beside it, staggered so neighbours do not collide. `ReferenceLine` is full height and its caption is fixed at the top. | `PinMarkers` with the `renderPin` escape hatch: a raw `<line>`, `ShapeGlyph` and a `<text>` (class `sui-chart__ref-label`), two rows staggered by index, name flipped to the left near the right edge. Kit: `calendar-strip.tsx`. **Raw SVG.** | `PinMarkers` props `label: (pin) => string` and `stem: boolean`, with the stagger and edge flip in the pure core (`labelPlacement` already knows how to avoid collisions). |
| 3 | **Range lanes for spans made of separated segments.** `packSpans` packs by a span's whole extent, so a range that crosses New Year (Jan 1–3 and Dec 20–31) claims the entire year and can share a lane with nothing. The sketch packs by segment, so every segment of one range sits in one lane and unrelated ranges still share. | The model packs lanes (`packLanes`, tested: the fewest lanes, one lane per range). The bench renders one `SpanLanes` per lane, each segment its own span (never overlapping within a lane, so `packSpans` puts them on one row). Kit: `calendar-strip.tsx`. | `packSpans` / `SpanLanes` packing by segments (a span occupies its segments, not its extent), or a `lane?: (datum) => number` on `SpanLanes` so a caller can supply the packing. |
| 4 | **A compact, labelled pick over plain values.** `CompactDropdown` wants string ids and has no label; `Select` is the form-size control, has no small size, and matches its value by option object. Every month, weekday, "which" and unit field on the page is one. | `ChoiceDropdown` (`fields.tsx`): a `TightStack` of a `TextSublabel` over `CompactDropdown`, mapping `value` to `String(value)` and back. | `Dropdown` props `label` and a generic value (`items: { value: V; label }[]`, `onChange(value: V)`), or `Select` `size="sm"`. |
| 5 | **A number field that only commits numbers.** `ThemedNumberInput` and `CurrencyInput` emit `undefined` when cleared; every caller here drops it. | `NumField` / `MoneyField` (`fields.tsx`): `size="sm"`, a label, and an `onChange` that ignores `undefined`. | `ThemedNumberInput` / `CurrencyInput` `onCommit(value: number)`, fired only for a real number. |
| 6 | **Dials that each scale to their own max, with a click-to-edit bound, and that report hover.** `GroupedMutationSliders` puts every dial on ONE shared domain (the widest range) and draws each dial's own `[0, max]` as the allowed box. The sketch scales each dial to its own max and lets the reader click the max bound to change it; it also cross-highlights a dial with its band on the strip. The grouped row has no entity-hover callback. | One measure per season; `range: [0, max]`, shared domain. The max is fixed example data. No max editing and no dial-to-strip hover link. | `GroupedMutationSliders` `perEntityDomain` (each dial's track is its own range), `onEntityHover(id \| null)`, and an `onRangeEdit` on the bound (`MarkedSlider` already has a range dialog in `createRangeDialog`, which the contract-scheduler bench uses from a button). |
| 7 | **"Selected" versus "pinned together."** In `GroupedMutationSliders` a name click toggles the selection, which also LINKS the pinned dials so they move together. Here a name click should only pick the season whose timing is shown. | `selected` is controlled to exactly the picked season, and `onSelectionChange` sets it, so nothing is ever pinned with anything else. | An `onEntityPick(id)` that names selection-for-editing separately from the link behaviour. |
| 8 | **Chart text scales with width.** `Chart`'s `responsive` scales the whole SVG, so tick and pin text shrinks on a phone. | Accepted for the bench. | A `Chart` that keeps text at a stated px size while the plot scales (measured, like `SpanLanes`' consumers do). |

## Differences from the sketch that follow from the above

- Bands take the series palette (slot per stacking rank), not the sketch's blue crew / green work hues.
- No hover dimming or hover link between a dial and its band; a click on the strip picks the topmost season on that day.
- No season names printed on the bands (only in each band's `<title>`).
- No dashed "seam" mark where a New Year range wraps; the two segments sit in the same lane and the range card lists them.
- Dials share one track domain (gap 6); the max bound is not clickable.
- The sketch's closing notes (the algebra, laws, open questions) are not rendered; the algebra and laws are the header of `seasonal-builder-model.ts` and the tests.
